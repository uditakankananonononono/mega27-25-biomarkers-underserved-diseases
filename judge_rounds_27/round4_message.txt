 TranscriptShift: study-disjoint and site-disjoint asthma-expression
 transfer audits
 Research report, September 25, 2026. This document is a candidate-specific expansion of the short PAPER.md, not a
 chapter counted again in the aggregate item-27 manuscript. Its first experiment is a negative test of transfer between two
 GEO cohorts in one parent publication. Its second is a separate negative within-study site transfer using another GEO
 series and microarray platform. Neither makes a clinical diagnostic claim.

 Abstract
 A model that classifies cases in a sample-random partition may rely on study conditions rather than disease signal. We used
 exact original GEO series matrices for GSE141623 (BAMSE, 256 blood-expression profiles) and GSE141631 (INMA, 201
 profiles), two separate cohorts under the same parent GSE141661. Phenotypes were parsed from per-sample asthma: 0/1
 characteristics. A panel of the first 1,000 valid probe rows was selected without phenotype inspection. Fixed logistic
 regression (C=0.1) with train-only median imputation and standardization was trained on one full study and evaluated on
 the other. AUC was 0.457 BAMSE to INMA and 0.521 in reverse, below the build-time exploratory >0.65 two-direction
 gate. An independent third GEO series, GSE110551 (156 adults across Wroclaw and Zurich), uses a different GPL570
 probe space. It therefore cannot validate the first fitted model. A separate, identically specified model trained on one site
 scored AUC 0.613 on the other in one direction and 0.545 in reverse. No tuned neural model was compared because the
 fixed linear baseline did not pass. The useful result is the fail-closed split and provenance audit, not a validated asthma
 classifier.

 1. Research question and practical use
 The question is whether a simple expression-based classifier holds when an entire study or site is withheld, not whether
 expression changes in asthma exist. The intended user is a researcher who needs to audit a proposed classification
 benchmark before relying on its sample-random performance. The code provides original-source manifests, phenotype
 extraction, a study-disjoint split, a second within-study site split, test-set uncertainty and explicit refusal to merge
 incompatible probe ID spaces. It does not output patient diagnoses.
 Study-level and site-level splits differ. BAMSE versus INMA isolates two cohorts processed on GPL17586, but the parent
 series and publication overlap. Wroclaw versus Zurich isolates locations within one independent series on GPL570, but not
 publication or laboratory processing. Neither split alone establishes generalization to a prospective hospital. The scopes
 remain separate in result files and throughout this report.

           GSE110551_series_matrix.txt.gz

 data/manifest.json and data/external_site_results.json record source SHA-256 hashes. Preparation scripts
 read !Sample_geo_accession and exact !Sample_characteristics_ch1 fields, not inferred labels from signal.
 data/cohorts.json and data/site_cohort.json retain each original sample accession and selected probe values.
 Full source matrices total about 190 MB and are not committed; each can be re-downloaded from its URL and checked

 BAMSE has 98 asthma-labeled samples and 158 controls, prevalence 0.3828. INMA has 36 cases and 165 controls,
 prevalence 0.1791. The third series has 78 asthma and 78 non-asthma adults. Wroclaw has 117 profiles (58 cases) and
 Zurich 39 (20 cases). These prevalence and recruitment differences are plausible sources of transfer failure. The GEO
 fields provide no independent clinical re-adjudication.

 3. Fixed feature construction and leakage control
 For each GPL17586 cohort, the parser takes the first 1,000 valid numeric probe rows in matrix order. Their exact
 intersection is 1,000 probe IDs. The choice is computational and independent of phenotype, not a biologically optimized
 gene signature. GPL570 likewise uses its first 1,000 valid rows, but there are zero probe IDs shared with the GPL17586
 panel. The data object stores GEO sample IDs; overlap checks find zero repeated samples between BAMSE and INMA and
 zero between those cohorts and GSE110551.
 Median imputation and standardization are inside a scikit-learn pipeline and fit only on the training study or site. Logistic
 regression has fixed C=0.1, max_iter=1,500 and seed 27. No features are selected by outcome on the test set. The platform
 difference is structural: an original model expecting the GPL17586 feature identity cannot be applied to GPL570 merely
 because both have 1,000 columns. Cross-platform gene mapping would require platform annotation, probe-to-gene
 disambiguation, common-gene aggregation and a prospective analysis plan; none was done here. The second experiment
 trains a new model on the independent platform rather than pretending the first was externally validated.

 4. Outcomes and gate
 The primary descriptive metric is held-out AUROC, the probability that a randomly chosen case scores above a randomly
 chosen control plus half of ties. Balanced accuracy at threshold 0.5 is secondary; it averages sensitivity and specificity. The
 build-time exploratory gate was AUC >0.65 in both directions of a cohort pair. This threshold was scoped during the build
 and is not a third-party preregistration. Within-study random sample splits are diagnostic comparators only, never
 substituted for the holdout. Neural models were deliberately not tested after the baseline failed because fitting a more
 flexible model to these outcomes would invite outcome-driven tuning.

 Both study directions and both site directions fail at point estimates. The random-sample AUCs within BAMSE and INMA
 were 0.5180 and 0.6055 respectively; these are diagnostics and not independent validation. No claim of a dramatic
 random-versus-study gap follows when one within-study AUC is itself weak. The most honest result is that this simple
 fixed panel fails, not that all asthma expression models must fail.

 5. Uncertainty and sensitivity
 uncertainty.py refits only the same fixed training pipelines to reproduce held-out scores. It then draws 2,000 class-
 stratified bootstrap samples of test subjects with replacement, without touching model parameters or the feature panel.
 Percentile intervals are listed above. This is conditional uncertainty for the chosen studies, phenotypes, frozen panel and
 model. It does not quantify platform harmonization error, selection of cohorts, label verification or a future site's case mix.
 The Zurich test interval is especially wide at n=39; its upper end crosses the exploratory threshold even though the point
 estimate and reverse direction fail. We do not reinterpret that upper interval as a passing gate.
 The sample-random comparator is vulnerable to within-study structure even when no subject is duplicated. Conversely,
 study/site splits introduce severe distribution differences that may depress true signal or expose nontransportability.
 Distinguishing those explanations requires additional independent data and planned covariate analyses. No post-hoc
 stratification by age, sex or obesity was used to declare success.

 7. Reproducibility
 From the repository root, install NumPy, scikit-learn and pytest. Run python3 projects/transcript_shift/
 prepare.py to retrieve and parse the two GPL17586 matrices, compare the printed SHA-256 values to committed data/
 manifest.json, and run python3 projects/transcript_shift/evaluate.py. For the independent site audit, run
 python3 projects/transcript_shift/external_site.py. Run python3 projects/transcript_shift/
 uncertainty.py for conditional AUC intervals, python3 projects/transcript_shift/study_report.py for
 cross-check tables, and python3 -m pytest -q projects/transcript_shift. The scripts make no account-
 dependent or paid-service calls. The committed extracted JSON supports model reproduction without re-downloading
 source matrices, but original-data provenance should be checked when publishing.

 8. Limitations and verdict
 The two primary cohorts are study-disjoint but publication-linked. The third is publication-distinct but evaluated only
 within its own site split. Phenotypes come from GEO submitter metadata, not a harmonized clinical adjudication. First-in-
 matrix probes are arbitrary; the panel may be biologically uninformative. Age, geography, recruitment, expression
 processing, disease prevalence and site batches are not adjusted. AUROC does not measure calibration or patient utility. No
 gene mechanism, biomarker nomination or clinical diagnosis is warranted. The result is a reproducible negative benchmark
 and an auditor for easy leakage, with a clear path to a genuinely independent hospital/endpoint-matched validation if
 original compatible expression and phenotype data become available.

 Independent Asthma BRIDGE assay, same-study random split only
 A fourth original GEO series, GSE285752, reports 954 whole-blood profiles in an array experiment distinct from the three
 earlier series. Its platform is GPL10558, not the GPL17586 probe space of the original BAMSE/INMA model; it cannot be
 called that model's external validation. We downloaded the original submitter metadata XLS and full ~685 MB compressed
 normalized expression matrix from the GEO supplementary URLs in data/bridge_protocol.json; the source
 SHA-256 digests and code are committed, while the large matrix is excluded from Git and can be fetched again. The
 metadata has 852 whole-blood asthma and 102 controls. Four sample rows lack IDAT filenames; there are no duplicated
 nonmissing filenames. The matrix has 1,535 total sample columns, each paired with a detection-p-value column.
 bridge_side_study.py joins the 954 whole-blood sample names exactly and takes the first 1,000 numeric ILMN probe
 rows without consulting labels.
 A local descriptive protocol was written before computing the result: one seed-27 stratified 75/25 sample split, training-
 only imputation/standardization and fixed logistic C=0.1. Its 715 train and 239 test samples include 639 and 213 asthma
 cases respectively. Held-out AUROC is 0.9074; average precision 0.9872 against test prevalence 0.8912; balanced
 accuracy at threshold 0.5 is 0.6829. This is a within-one-study random sample split with extreme class imbalance, not an
 independent hospital holdout, not a matched published benchmark and not transfer of either fitted GPL17586 model. A
 post-result confounding audit of the original metadata found controls aged 23.95-31.50 years (median 25.90), versus cases
 aged 5.17-31.65 years (median 10.03). All 102 controls but only 102 of 852 cases lie within the shared 23.95-31.50 age
 range; sex also differs (controls 62 F/40 M, cases 351 F/501 M). On the same frozen split, an age-only logistic model
 scored AUC 0.9497, higher than the expression model, although age was not an input to that expression model. Post-hoc
 restriction of the original held-out predictions to the overlapping age range leaves only 42 subjects (16 cases) and gives
 expression AUC 0.6707; this tiny, selected subset is descriptive and not a fresh validation, age-adjusted performance claim
 or benchmark pass. The apparently high all-age random-split AUC may reflect developmental differences and recruitment
 composition, so it cannot be interpreted as disease-specific signal. It does not reverse the earlier negative cross-study AUCs
 or meet the two-direction >0.65 transfer gate. No clinical diagnostic claim or cross-platform model mapping follows. Exact
 probe/metadata source hashes, counts and refusal scope are in data/bridge_result.json.
