13.1 Why the eight build verdicts differ
ToxinAudit-LabelScope (#3) retains its original 26-entry panel and expands to 120 separately fetched reviewed
UniProt entries under two fixed EC queries. Forty-one expanded entries have direct target-EC experimental
citations; the earlier panel had twelve. The absence of such a citation in the others cannot serve as a negative
functional assay: an unmeasured protein may still catalyze the reaction. The classifier refuses to fit until
independent measured negatives and homolog-disjoint controls exist. QuickGO, InterPro and ChEMBL add
contextual annotation and target identity; UniRef50 maps all 120 selected entries to 73 half-identity clusters, 19
containing multiple panel entries. These checks are not biochemical null results or assay-disjoint folds. This is a
useful failure-before-modeling control, but no predictive AUROC or novel enzyme-function finding follows.
Source: projects/toxin_audit/accessions.json, results.json and external_checks.json.
CodonContext (#4) takes a candidate coding sequence and checks translation preservation, reading frame,
terminal stop, GC/motif and window constraints. A first-120 E. coli panel separately retrieves 120 UniProt
proteins, mapping them to 120 translated CDS features from a single fetched RefSeq chromosome; it is not 120
separately fetched nucleotide accessions. A PSI protein-expression dataset supports an exploratory accession-
prefix split with AUC 0.659, but is not a same-protein synonymous construct test. Angov et al. 2008 provides five
paired wild-type/codon-harmonized yield rows, without full paired CDS sequences or comparable CAI; it checks
experiment feasibility, not a held-out CAI-gain benchmark. The separate published NAR 2023 mRFP workbook
supplies 1,459 sequence/fluorescence rows for one protein and one host, crosschecked against a downstream CSV
as one evidence chain. Its exploratory first-eight-codon fixed feature had a held-out within-study Pearson r of
0.722 versus 0.521 for GC features, but a post-result source-header holdout had macro R2 -0.305 and two
negative folds. Those are not 1,459 independent datasets, a matched published benchmark or a new biological
discovery. The panel does not supply paired constructs measured in an expression host. Its CAI arithmetic
therefore cannot answer whether synonymous changes increase yield, even if a constraint checker can reject
impossible or unwanted sequences. This is distinct from PhosGene despite shared P07102 context and must not
count that overlap as an independent expression experiment. Source: projects/codon_context/README.md and
evidence_ledger.json.
GraphHoldout (#5) uses 556 curated CTD gene-disease-PMID evidence rows with PubMed publication dates. It
represents an edge and its reverse as one canonical pair and excludes shared paper IDs across the retrospective
time split. Of 114 later novel associations, 106 involve genes absent from the earlier graph; only eight positives
are rankable at all. Asthma 2/2 among the eight cannot validate a general ranker when the other disease groups
yield 0/5 and 0/1. The 2026 CTD snapshot is filtered by article date, not historical curation date; current Open
Targets context can overlap the same papers. The observed cold-start limit and leakage refusal are results;
prospective gene-disease prediction is not. Source: projects/graph_holdout/data/results.json and REPORT.md.
TranscriptShift (#6) downloads expression matrices for two GEO cohort accessions, uses a fixed 1,000-probe
linear model and trains on an entire study before testing on the other. AUCs of 0.457 and 0.521 fail its two-
direction gate. An independent publication supplies GSE110551 with a different platform and explicit site
metadata. The old fitted probe model cannot be applied as if probes were harmonized; a separate fixed baseline
gives Wroclaw-to-Zurich AUC 0.613 and reverse 0.545. Conditional bootstrap AUC intervals for the original
directions are 0.350-0.560 and 0.448-0.596; they do not cover future hospitals or clinical phenotype adjudication.
