E9. Beta-binomial posterior
The Beta update assumes exchangeable Bernoulli trials for the chosen narrow endpoint. The five idea builds were
selected deliberately and are not exchangeable draws from all project ideas or awards. A posterior on runnable
construction cannot be transported to discovery or award odds.

E10. Service and dataset units
The tool denominator is independent scientific services with actually used output, not API calls. The accession
denominator is separate direct entry retrievals used in a scored audit, not all IDs in a bulk query. Their units
differ: 120 entries from one UniProt service satisfy an entry-count condition while adding one, not 120, to a
scientific-service ledger.


14. Reproducibility, limitations and next valid tests
The research record has a defined scope for each outcome, not one pooled pass/fail label. The venom sequence
baseline uses reviewed snake-toxin entries, a UniProt keyword and one family ontology supplied by the same
database. It is meaningful to report the random versus family-held-out ranking gap and the direct-entry test
counts; it would be wrong to transport those numbers to venom potency or antivenom selection. The balanced
120-entry selection creates a 50% positive fraction in each target family by construction, while the original 1,814-
entry cohort has 542 keyword positives. AP, threshold sensitivity and any predicted probability should therefore
be interpreted against each sample-specific prevalence rather than as a clinical risk score.
The feature map is deliberately simple: contiguous amino-acid 3-mers and logistic weights. Exact-sequence
collapse removes identical duplicate rows but not homologs, and the follow-up similarity audit shows why
random folds are easier. A family category fed directly to another classifier reaches AUROC 0.87929 on those
random folds, which is striking annotation signal but cannot prove which sequence motifs the first classifier used.
A genuine independent test would preselect experimentally measured activities, freeze a protein homolog
clustering method before outcome inspection, separate training and test by both family and measurement
publication, and report calibration as well as discrimination. No such cohort has been obtained in this manuscript.
For PhosGene, the AppA reference sequence and codon table are reproducible public records, and translation
conservation is an exact software check. CAI 1.0 after choosing the maximally weighted synonym for every
amino acid says only that the optimizer follows its reference counts. In an expression experiment the appropriate
unit would be paired constructs in a named host with matched promoter, secretion system, culture conditions,
protein yield and enzyme activity. A host mismatch, mRNA folding change or secretion failure could reverse the
arithmetic ranking. The pig fecal-P endpoint additionally needs feed composition, active enzyme exposure and
absorption measurements. No amount of resampling the toy mass-balance equation can replace those original
assays.
The 2018 dietary-pig and earlier Enviropig reports are contexts, not exchangeable calibration targets. The former
gives diet-specific fecal-P decreases of 45.8% and 34.8% for a multi-enzyme setup; the earlier construct reports
up to 75% under different biology. Selecting model inputs to land within these numbers after seeing them would
be target leakage. The evidence validator reports ten missing or unsupported input dimensions and a null
phenotype for the actual packet. A sensible next test would gather measurements for every dimension in one
specified feeding design, assess source relevance before the model is run, and leave any transfer assumption
explicit. Even a numerical agreement would then need uncertainty and an independent animal comparison, not
just a plausible point estimate.
The award-corpus analysis has a different identification problem. Its 125 rows are awarded project listings, not a
registry of all submissions. Titles are a lossy summary of methods, category boundaries vary, and repeated top-
award mentions were deduplicated by year/project code. A title-term frequency can guide which ideas to inspect;
it cannot say which technique improves award probability. A valid rubric study would recruit independent blinded
scorers for the same anonymized idea briefs and compare scores with actual later results, retaining both failures

A shared denominator cannot discharge candidate-specific resource requirements. VenomFunction has 120 direct
accession reads that were scored, but only six distinct counted scientific service uses; PhosGene has eleven
distinct scientific services and an exploratory 129-entry direct protein-family provenance audit, clearing the
narrow accession-count threshold but not an experimentally validated phytase or pig-phenotype gate. Candidate
#7 has 120 BioSample records, yet the cohort lacks a multi-hospital interpreted R/S comparison and no genome
predictor has been validated. Candidates #3 and #4 now have 120 separately retrieved protein entries for their
narrow audits, but not their measured-function or synonymous-expression benchmarks; #5-6 and #8-10 retain
source and accession deficits. Repeated endpoint requests, mirrored annotation services, a bulk table with many
IDs, and a bibliography of tools are not forty materially used biological services for any one project. These
shortfalls remain visible in the ledgers and cannot be repaired by relabeling repositories or supplement pages as
evidence.
The paper and companion supplement have complementary purposes. The main text has the full study question,
evaluation designs, measured results, failure analysis, ten-build status and limits. The supplement is an audit trail:
all 120 toxin entry scores, the 125 listed awards and each attempted external service endpoint. A reader should
use the main paper to understand the claims and the supplement to verify individual rows, then inspect the cited
JSON/TSV and source URLs for exact sequences, dates and retrieval hashes. The supplement does not add
independent experiments, and a long appendix is not a substitute for valid data or methods.
