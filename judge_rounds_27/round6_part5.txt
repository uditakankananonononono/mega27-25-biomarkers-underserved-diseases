The unresolved pig-phenotype reproduction led to a concrete within-PhosGene pivot rather than an invented
positive effect. The new identifiability validator demands a numerical value, unit, source URL, organism, assay or
feeding condition, and transfer rationale for every parameter used to turn AppA activity into a predicted fecal-P
effect. Its requirement table is fixed in protocols/phosgene_identifiability_2026-09-25.md, and the
implementation is phosgene/identifiability.py. A non-pig assay cannot silently stand in for pig-lumen physiology:
the provided rationale must be substantial, but a substantial statement alone would not scientifically validate
transfer. Owner/source review would still be required before interpreting a calculation as an animal result.

9.3 Exploratory AppA-family provenance and transfer audit
To make better use of public sequence evidence without pretending it supplies missing pig physiology, a follow-
up queried reviewed UniProtKB entries carrying InterPro IPR000560, a family cross-reference verified on the
direct AppA P07102 entry. The query returned 89 named entries; all 89 were retrieved at separate accession
JSON endpoints, checked for exact primary accession, reviewed status and family cross-reference, and used in an
untuned amino-acid 3-mer Jaccard comparison with P07102. Each record preserves source URL, SHA-256,
sequence, organism, name, EC evidence and the explicit refusal to transfer its rate to pig gut. This is an
exploratory source-scope study; the local plan preceded entry fetching but followed the original pig-model failure.
Only 16 of the 89 direct records are explicitly named phytases. 34 carry at least one EC annotation with an
experimental PubMed citation, which is still not a common-substrate or pig-lumen assay. The maximum non-
anchor 3-mer Jaccard with AppA is 0.07798. A broad family match, name or low descriptive k-mer overlap does
not establish equivalent catalytic products, secretion or in-vivo rate; these entries cannot calibrate the fecal-P
sandbox. A second, pre-stated unreviewed bacterial phytase-label stratum added 40 disjoint direct entries. All 40
carry explicit unreviewed phytase names, but zero experimental EC PubMed citations; maximum AppA 3-mer
Jaccard is 0.13043. The combined narrow family-provenance audit uses 129 direct-fetched protein records (89
reviewed, 40 unreviewed), which meets an accession-count condition for that annotation tool only. It does not

10. Calibration and failure modes
A Beta(1,1) prior with three selected retrospective runnable builds produces Beta(4,1), posterior mean 0.800 and
equal-tail interval [0.398,0.994]. Counting the two newer runnable candidates would yield Beta(6,1), mean 0.857
and interval [0.541,0.996] for the narrowly defined end point of runnable construction. These values are not a
biological success rate, a probability of winning an award, or evidence the rubric predicts discovery. An
unassessable animal outcome cannot be relabeled a success just because a CLI runs.
                                 P(q | s,n) = Beta(1+s, 1+n-s) under a uniform Beta(1,1) prior.
                                        E[q | s,n] = (1+s)/(2+n); with s=n=3, E[q]=4/5.
Selection of high-scored ideas inflates apparent build completion. With only two new post-protocol tests and no
blinding, weakest-criterion calibration is not statistically assessable. The independent rater template is blank.
VenomFunction-2 shows a useful failure of a homolog-sensitive split, while PhosGene fails a source-parameter
sufficiency test. Both are legitimate bounded outcomes with different scopes.
