13.1 Why the eight build verdicts differ
ToxinAudit-LabelScope (#3) retains its original 26-entry panel and expands to 120 separately fetched reviewed
UniProt entries under two fixed EC queries. Forty-one expanded entries have direct target-EC experimental
citations; the earlier panel had twelve. The absence of such a citation in the others cannot serve as a negative
functional assay: an unmeasured protein may still catalyze the reaction. The classifier refuses to fit until
independent measured negatives and homolog-disjoint controls exist. QuickGO, InterPro and ChEMBL add
contextual annotation and target identity; UniRef50 maps all 120 selected entries to 73 half-identity clusters, 19
containing multiple panel entries. These checks are not biochemical null results or assay-disjoint folds. This is a
useful failure-before-modeling control, but no predictive AUROC or novel enzyme-function finding follows.
Source: projects/toxin_audit/accessions.json, results.json and external_checks.json.
CodonContext (#4) takes a candidate coding sequence and checks translation preservation, reading frame,
terminal stop, GC/motif and window constraints. A first-120 E. coli panel separately retrieves 120 UniProt
proteins, mapping them to 120 translated CDS features from a single fetched RefSeq chromosome; it is not 120
separately fetched nucleotide accessions. A PSI protein-expression dataset supports an exploratory accession-
prefix split with AUC 0.659, but is not a same-protein synonymous construct test. Angov et al. 2008 provides five
paired wild-type/codon-harmonized yield rows, without full paired CDS sequences or comparable CAI; it checks
experiment feasibility, not a held-out CAI-gain benchmark. The separate published NAR 2023 mRFP workbook
supplies 1,459 sequence/fluorescence rows for one protein and one host, crosschecked against a downstream CSV
as one evidence chain. Its exploratory first-eight-codon fixed feature had a held-out within-study Pearson r of
0.722 versus 0.521 for GC features, but a post-result source-header holdout had macro R2 -0.305 and two
negative folds. Those are not 1,459 independent datasets, a matched published benchmark or a new biological
discovery. The panel does not supply paired constructs measured in an expression host. Its CAI arithmetic
therefore cannot answer whether synonymous changes increase yield, even if a constraint checker can reject
impossible or unwanted sequences. This is distinct from PhosGene despite shared P07102 context and must not
count that overlap as an independent expression experiment. Source: projects/codon_context/README.md and
evidence_ledger.json.
GraphHoldout (#5) uses 556 curated CTD gene-disease-PMID evidence rows with PubMed publication dates. It
represents an edge and its reverse as one canonical pair and excludes shared paper IDs across the retrospective
time split. Of 114 later novel associations, 106 involve genes absent from the earlier graph; only eight positives
are rankable at all. Asthma 2/2 among the eight cannot validate a general ranker when the other disease groups
yield 0/5 and 0/1. The 2026 CTD snapshot is filtered by article date, not historical curation date; current Open
Targets context can overlap the same papers. The observed cold-start limit and leakage refusal are results;
prospective gene-disease prediction is not. Source: projects/graph_holdout/data/results.json and REPORT.md.
TranscriptShift (#6) downloads expression matrices for two GEO cohort accessions, uses a fixed 1,000-probe
linear model and trains on an entire study before testing on the other. AUCs of 0.457 and 0.521 fail its two-
direction gate. An independent publication supplies GSE110551 with a different platform and explicit site
metadata. The old fitted probe model cannot be applied as if probes were harmonized; a separate fixed baseline
gives Wroclaw-to-Zurich AUC 0.613 and reverse 0.545. Conditional bootstrap AUC intervals for the original
directions are 0.350-0.560 and 0.448-0.596; they do not cover future hospitals or clinical phenotype adjudication.

MicrobeTransfer (#7) begins from a 6,463-row resistance-gene index, not an isolate-level susceptible/resistant
trial. The follow-up fetches 120 BioSample XML records by exact identifiers and records 442 explicit R/S assay
rows. All 30 isolates with interpreted R/S share one collector, so a hospital-disjoint test set does not exist.
Assembly links for 29 of these isolates are not genome sequences fetched and used in a predictor. The input
contract and abstention rule are operational software, but no clinical susceptibility performance is measured.
Matching gene annotations to AST labels without isolate/drug/breakpoint provenance would falsely promote
catalog entries into phenotypes. Source: builds/microbe_transfer/ncbi_ast_results.json and README.md.
PhageHostScope (#8) starts from measured lytic host-range pairs, making its data unit different from a docking
score. It uses source-provided host and phage trees, holds out groups on both axes and quarantines pairs crossing
only one axis. Its within-study AUC 0.709 on 1,120 jointly held-out pairs narrowly crosses an exploratory
threshold, but those trees, aliases and group cutoffs were not independently reconstructed or externally confirmed.
A separate Klebsiella cohort of 12,970 measured pairs cannot simply accept the E. coli host-tree distance model; a
BASEL E. coli source adds 396 measured lysis records but no verified phage/host identity join to the Paris
training pairs; absent comparable host representation, the external AUC is intentionally refused. The result is a
bounded same-study benchmark, not cross-species host-range prediction. Source:
builds/phage_host_scope/results.json and external_results.json.
RNAEvidence (#9) separates curated record grades and publication IDs in a historical 2015 miRTarBase
snapshot, quarantining repeated miRNA-gene pairs. A simple assay-propensity comparator yields AUC 0.647 on
454 same-database records, but tested nonfunctional records are selected and cannot represent the unbiased space
of all true negatives. The independent miRBench CLASH/eCLIP files instead test a fixed 7-mer sequence rule at
the site level; 858 of 954 eCLIP test rows share gene clusters with training context, and two exact site-pair
overlaps were quarantined; that is a different endpoint, not external validation of the historical curated gene-pair
model. Its risk is endpoint substitution, mirrored literature and constructed negative sites. Neither AUC is a
prospective target-interaction ranking result. Source: builds/rna_evidence/results.json, README.md and
mirbench_results.json.
SpatialArtifactGuard (#10) checks all 15,611 spot barcodes and metadata counts against a downloaded spatial
gene matrix and image-position list, then evaluates a patient-out RNA-QC/center-pixel color baseline. That matrix
audit establishes row linkage and count agreement, not precise histology pixel registration or an external site. A
pre-stated 37-pixel image-coordinate shift control lowered all six AUCs, supporting a spatial-registration
dependence within the same atlas but not an external-site gate. The apparent fold AUCs include groups with only
four or one artifact spot; averaging such unstable folds would hide denominator fragility. The atlas is one source
across six slides and labels can reflect local annotation practices. The scientific gate remains external-site
validation with enough artifacts per held-out patient, not a spot-level random split. Source:
builds/spatial_artifact_guard/gene_alignment_results.json, results.json and README.md.

13.2 Cross-candidate interpretation
The eight software builds fail in materially different ways. Some refuse a prediction because the necessary
endpoint or linkage was never established (#3, #4, #7); some run a fixed benchmark and preserve poor transfer or
limited coverage (#5, #6, #9, #10); #8 has a within-study score but no comparable external test. A negative AUC
and a missing validation cohort should not be merged into one failed-performance category. The separate service
ledger uses data providers per build, not files or Python packages: #3-#6 count 5, 5, 3, 1; #7-#10 now count 4, 3,
2, 1. No candidate may borrow another candidate accession cohort to claim 120, and none approaches 40 services.
The program result is a transparent set of runnable audits and demonstrated evaluation traps, not ten established
biological discoveries.
Supplementary material: exact accession predictions, the full award listing, and endpoint-level service provenance
are in MEGA27-27-research-supplement.pdf. The main paper stands alone for methods, results, limitations and
the candidate inventory.
