!Sample_treatment_protocol_ch1 = Cells were re-stimulated prior to FACS staining using 30ng/mL of PMA and 1ug/mL of ionomycin and incubated for 4h at 37C with 5% CO2. Following incubation, IFNg-secreting and IL-17-secreting cells were fluorescently labelled using IFNg Secretion Assay – Detection Kit (FITC; 130-090-433, Miltenyi Biotec, Canada) and IL-17 Secretion Assay – Detection Kit (PE; 130-094-537, Miltenyi Biotec, Canada), as per manufacturer’s protocol. This was conducted to avoid permeabilizing cells to allow for downstream analysis. During staining, cells were also labelled with CD3 (300312) and CD4 (300511) extracellular Abs, as well as the viability dye (423102), all products were purchased from BioLegend unless otherwise stated. Following washing, cells were resuspended in 2% FACS buffer for FACS sorting. Cells were sorted into viable populations of Th17 (CD3+CD4+IFNg-IL-17+), Th1/17 (CD3+CD4+IFNg+IL-17+), and Th1 cells (CD3+CD4+IFNg+IL-17-) in collection media, consisting of 50% FBS and 50% phenol-free RPMI 1640 medium (11835030, ThermoFisher, Canada). Samples were stored on ice, added to respective DNA LoBind Tubes (EP022431021, Millipore Sigma, Canada), and centrifuged at 400RCF for 10mins at 4C before resuspending pellets in 300uL of RNAprotect Cell Reagent (76526, Qiagen, Canada). Cells were stored at -80C prior to RNA isolation.
!Sample_growth_protocol_ch1 = Prior to sorting cells (FACS), PBMCs were briefly thawed in a water bath at 37C. Cell suspensions were added to warm RPMI media with 10% FBS and centrifuged at 400RCF for 10mins at 20C prior to resuspending in 2% FACS buffer and counting cells. CD4+ T cells were then isolated using a commercially available kit (17952, StemCell, Canada), as per manufacturer’s protocol. CD4+ T cells were washed and resuspended in warm TexMACS media (130-097-196, Miltenyi Biotec, USA) prior to seeding in 96-well plates at 1x10^6 cells/well.
!Sample_molecule_ch1 = total RNA
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = RNA was isolated using RNeasy Micro Kit (74004, Qiagen, Canada), as per manufacturer’s guidelines. RNA purity was assessed using the Nanodrop Spectrophotometer (ThermoFisher, Canada) prior to storage at -80C.
!Sample_extract_protocol_ch1 = Total RNA purity was assessed prior to library preparation. RNA samples were processed at the McGill Genome Centre (Quebec, Canada) using the NEBNext Ultra II Directional RNA Library Prep Kit (New England Biolabs) with poly (A) mRNA enrichment. Libraries were generated according to standard NEBNext protocols including mRNA capture, fragmentation, first- and second-strand cDNA synthesis, end repair, adaptor ligation, and PCR amplification. Libraries underwent Ampure XP bead clean-up and quality assessment (LibQC), including removal of primer dimer fragments, before pooling and sequencing on an Illumina NovaSeq X Plus platform using paired-end reads.
