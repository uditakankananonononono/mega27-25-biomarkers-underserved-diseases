# Fibromyalgia: proposed novelty test, not a result

**Current verdict:** No new named biological discovery, validated new method, or demonstrated same-task advantage. This is an unproven research direction, not a novelty claim or finished project.

## Falsifiable candidate contribution
A blood signal that transports across pre-treatment cohorts after controlling blood-cell mixture and medication rather than reflecting a cohort-specific shift.

## What would establish or refute it
Lock analytes and direction in training blood cohort, compare on independent untreated donor blood against a published model, then use cell-composition and sex sensitivity without recasting post-hoc subsets as replication. Predeclare the endpoint, exclusion rules, analysis code and comparator before examining the held-out outcome; retain null and negative results. If the effect fails, the proposed contribution fails rather than becoming a post-hoc new marker.

## Why present evidence falls short
The original GSE67311 FM discovery includes ten cases concurrently labeled chronic fatigue syndrome and six FM cases with missing CFS status; a healthy-control contrast cannot establish FM specificity against ME/CFS. The eight 2015 discovery genes reverse in a 2024 whole-blood deposit; 2024 signatures were already published. Source lead: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE221921. A source lead is not proof of novelty, matching participants, or clinical utility.
