# Shared-core identifier units, September 27, 2026

`python3 scripts/audit_manifest_units.py` reads the committed `results/dataset_manifest.csv` and the older `results/gsm_to_study.csv`. It asserts unique accession IDs, checks all old mappings are in the manifest, infers only the 180 newly included GSM mappings from explicit GSE roles (and the documented GSE28242 P7 series crosswalk), and emits `results/gsm_to_study_complete.csv`. It does not fetch or analyze expression.

Current manifest has **1,508 distinct records**, including **1,382 GSM specimen identifiers**, **89 GSE series identifiers**, **26 GPL platforms**, and **11 ontology/network metadata identifiers**. The old 1,202-row map was stale by 180 GSMs added September 25-26; this explains the old paper's 1,328 total exactly. The completed map has 1,382 GSM-to-GSE edges referencing 43 parent GSE series; these 43 are represented by specimens in the manifest, not 43 new studies in addition to the 89 GSE IDs. One mapped parent, GSE127831 (28 used leishmaniasis GSMs), is missing as its own accession row. This is a manifest completeness hold; do not silently add that accession without a source-use record. The other 46 GSE identifiers have no GSM mapping here, and an accession row alone cannot say if each represents an independent study or deduplicated reuse.

Counts are IDs, not people, independent cohorts or genuine external-service uses. Some GSMs are repeated libraries or paired samples; the study's independent participant count needs a separate disease-specific audit. The old prose's 1,328/1,202 was accurate for the older map snapshot but no longer matches the committed manifest and must not be presented as a current count.
