## What is known and unknown

GSE213363 comprises 112 blood MethylationEPIC arrays: donor IDs 1-56 each occur once as suffix `A` and once as suffix `B`, with a consistent GEO arm label within each pair (30 resistance, 26 continuous aerobic). The study paper describes pre- and post-16-week blood collection, but the GEO metadata and the paper's actual supplement never map `A` or `B` to before/after. **Arm labels are public and observed; temporal labels are not verified.** There is no donor-linked clinical phenotype table in these sources and no untreated PCOS arm in this deposition. See [GEO](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE213363), [paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10802204/), [supplement package](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/europepmc/webservices/rest/PMC10802204/supplementaryFiles), and [provenance audit](ORIENTATION_PROVENANCE_HUNT.md). The publication's data-availability citation to GSE213366 appears mismatched: that GEO accession names a different sperm study. This series is methylation, not transcriptomics.
