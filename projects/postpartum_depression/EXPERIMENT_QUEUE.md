# PPD next experiments, with stop rules

1. Resolve GSE290313 donor crosswalk from a primary supplementary sample sheet or depositor-authored mapping. The available full library token and `DE##` prefix cannot identify a person: 35 prefixes recur and 15 are case/control discordant. Stop before any patient-independent split unless an explicit donor identifier joins all selected GSMs.
2. GSE44132 technical and clinical stratification. Prespecify whether the target is future PPD in antenatally euthymic women versus all mood-disorder patients; record prenatal depression and array/experimental batches. Exclude the five PR01-084 technical-control arrays from person counts. Compare the published HP1BP3/TTC9B model on a truly held-out cohort, not within its derivation GSE.
3. GSE335141 temporal within-person question. Separately test the T0-to-T4 change among 41 women with paired models and appropriate covariates, rather than treating 82 specimens as 82 patients. T4 outcome and measurement cannot masquerade as an early prospective predictor. Any top CpG must survive multiplicity, platform-level probe QC and an independent cohort.
4. Cross-platform CpG transport is not yet a validation experiment: 382,794 canonical `cg` prefixes intersect, but EPICv2 suffix and probe-design equivalence must be checked against both array annotation manifests, then timing, specimen and outcome compatibility established. If either fails, report a boundary rather than an AUC.
5. Extend only with real human PPD case/control omics sources. Exclude mouse postpartum-brain, lactation drug-perturbation, heroin cortex and preeclampsia keyword matches already triaged. Record per-GSM source/hash, participant token, diagnosis and draw timing; count series, specimens and people separately.

All discoveries need an independent positive endpoint, not simply a metric over 120 nested accession IDs. Negative screens remain in their audit tables; final deliverable can redirect to a positive validated result only if evidence supports it.
