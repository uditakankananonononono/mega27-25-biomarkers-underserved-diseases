# GSE335141 longitudinal PPD methylation: source and prior-art hold

Reviewed September 26, 2026. This is a metadata/prior-art audit, not a new outcome analysis or disease-project completion.

The GEO [GSE335141 series](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE335141) was previously screened and its downloaded series SOFT is preserved in `sources/GSE335141_series_soft.txt`. It deposits 82 GSM libraries for 41 women, each sampled at delivery and 12 weeks postpartum (17 PPD and 24 controls), with a processed beta matrix and raw IDAT archive. The matching [April 2026 bioRxiv preprint](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.04.03.716376v1) already reports the longitudinal immune-epigenetic remodeling contrast, greater heterogeneity among PPD participants, and exploratory stable methylation loci. Those are prior art, not a new result to rediscover. The preprint also explicitly reports that no individual CpG achieved genome-wide significance in direct PPD/control tests and cautions that its marker candidates require replication.

**Source inconsistency requiring audit:** the main text repeatedly states 17 PPD/24 control women (41 total), yet the rendered Table 1 caption says 18 PPD/19 controls (37 total). This may be a clinical-table subset or an error; the excerpt does not adjudicate which individuals underlie each analysis. Do not assume a single patient denominator for clinical covariate modeling, and do not import any preprint-reported candidate CpGs as our independent discovery. The downloaded series SOFT has 82 GSM IDs but is not a patient-by-library crosswalk by itself; inspect the individual sample records and matrix headers before any paired model.

The author's longitudinal methylation finding offers an unusual *design* lead but is not an untouched external validation cohort for a CpG signature already selected on the same participants. The prior two-CpG GSE44132 question is a different prospective target and cannot be declared validated by matching only disease names. If a new question is proposed here, first establish patient ID/timepoint pairing, phenotype onset timing, clinical denominator, assay QC and a published same-task comparator; register a distinct endpoint and split before reading beta values. The published within-cohort outcome means a de novo nominal locus scan cannot honestly be called unseen discovery merely because our code is new.

Current action: provenance hold. No value inspection, accession increment, new marker, benchmark beat or judge-round novelty fix claimed.
