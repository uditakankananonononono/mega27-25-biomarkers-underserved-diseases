# Postpartum depression - proposed separate project

Status: project scaffold and gate audit only, not a finished paper or a positive benchmark. The parent repository is shared core; this directory must hold a disease-specific protocol, accession and external-service evidence ledger, reproducible results and a 50-page substantive paper before its gates can be claimed.

Current disease-tagged manifest records: 51 = 2 GSE studies + 48 nested GSM samples + 1 other. These are record units, not independent datasets or patients. The shared 40-service and 49-page PDF do not transfer as automatic per-project passes. Benchmark/discovery endpoint is open.

Disease-specific documents and source logs are not yet split from shared core; use the shared source code and result filenames by disease as leads, then verify original record attribution before copying.

## Screened, not used: recent methylation deposit

The directly fetched GEO series SOFT for [GSE335141](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE335141) lists 82 unique GSM identifiers, whole-blood EPICv2 methylation at two postpartum draws from 41 mothers (17 diagnosed PPD, 24 controls). Its source text is retained under `sources/`. A later exploratory T0 beta-matrix screen found zero BH-FDR<0.05 probes; no clinical validation was run, and these 82 specimens are deliberately **not** in the used manifest or its three PPD records. They are two repeated samples per mother, and methylation is a different modality from the expression ranking task. A future PPD project can investigate the cohort without asserting 82 independent datasets, a same-task accuracy benchmark, or a named new marker. The [2021 PPD blood-expression publication](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41398-021-01270-5) and [2025 pregnancy/postpartum transcript study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41380-025-03068-z) supply clinical and novelty context, not newly used cohort records by themselves.

## Individually verified used postpartum whole-blood cohort

The earlier program analysis had already used [GSE45603](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE45603), but its 48 analyzed GSMs were not yet in the accession manifest. We fetched all 48 individual GEO sample SOFT records, compared exact titles, postpartum timepoint, PPD/euthymic/control condition and GPL10558 platform with the committed series matrix and label file, and checked all 48 distinct person tokens and finite expression columns. The source-URL/hash crosswalk is in `sources/GSE45603_used_sample_crosswalk.csv`; it has 16 PPD cases and 32 euthymic/control postpartum people. Only these 48 expression-used GSMs were added, bringing PPD to 51 accession records. The separate GSE290313 postpartum analysis remains a real used series but its 119 selected GSMs have not been individually verified and are not silently counted. GSE335141 remains screened, with an exploratory T0 methylation screen but no expression-model validation. This is better dataset provenance, not a new positive benchmark or a novel PPD marker.

## GSE290313 postpartum library-to-person mapping pause

A separate existing P6 analysis uses 119 GSE290313 postpartum, SSRI-unexposed GSM libraries (35 labeled "depressive symptoms only postpartum", 84 controls). We independently fetched all 119 [individual GEO sample records](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE290313) and matched their title, source group, timepoint, SSRI field, GPL24676 platform and unique full library token to the committed P6 labels; source URLs and response hashes are in `sources/GSE290313_used_sample_crosswalk.csv`. **119 distinct library tokens are not proof of 119 distinct people.** The DE-number prefix repeats across records (80 distinct prefixes); some repeats are case/control discordant, for example DE21 and DE69. This prefix may be a recycled sample identifier, not necessarily a donor key. The associated [peer-reviewed paper](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12436180/) reports 188 women with postpartum RNA samples across its broader analysis (versus 184 GEO records explicitly titled postpartum), which makes 80 DE prefixes an implausible universal person key, but does **not** itself provide the exact donor-to-GSM crosswalk for our 119 selected libraries. Until original mapping resolves it, we do not use prefixes to collapse people or claim patient-independent validation. The 119 GSMs are individually source-verified as expression-used libraries but remain outside the conservative per-disease manifest pending the participant-unit audit; the P6 expression effects remain descriptive. No reported clinical classifier or novelty result is rescued by assuming uniqueness.
## PPD methylation source expansion (2026-09-26)

The 51 earlier disease-tagged accession records were largely expression-oriented. We separately fetched and analyzed two publicly deposited human methylation cohorts. They add 137 distinct GSM accession **records** under two new GSEs (139 accession records total), but **not** 139 independent datasets or participants, and neither cohort validates an RNA classifier. Across all three source studies the 51 + 139 = 190 accession records include four GSE studies (GSE45603, GSE290313, GSE44132, GSE335141), with the existing ontology record separate. This statement must be recalculated against the live manifest before use: the conservative manifest presently lists only 51, of which two are GSE and one is an ontology record. Two extra GSE plus 137 new GSM means 190 total, not 120 independent studies. GSE290313's 119 libraries remain excluded pending person mapping.

- [GSE44132](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE44132): 55 unique GPL13534 array GSMs, but five are technical repeats of the same PR01-084 person, giving 51 unique source patient tokens. Antenatal blood methylation with prospectively observed postpartum-depression label: 23 later PPD, 27 controls after omitting all five technical controls. The source-matched 55-column GEO beta matrix (SHA256 `b2d715ec45c250426c8b2a4e5317bf589f581f8425d1101d9f6d752ba6dc19b9`) yielded 483,266 finite probes, with zero BH-FDR<0.05 under exploratory case-control Welch tests. The crosswalk records source URL/hash, title, platform, original phenotype and patient token per sample.
- [GSE335141](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE335141): 82 unique GPL33022 array GSMs are T0 and T4 specimens from just 41 mothers, 17 PPD and 24 healthy. Every GSM's source `!Sample_description` was bound to one of the 82 columns in the normalized beta matrix (SHA256 `13002634be22d3b58529402b07b89a57610479f18f3208ebd8ce94c6fc12b5b2`). A T0-only one-mother-one-sample exploratory comparison retained 880,068 complete finite probes; none had BH-FDR<0.05. T4 was not used as an independent mother or as a predictive feature.

Files in `sources/` give the 137-row per-record crosswalk, full-source series snapshots, audit summaries and exploratory top-probe tables. Primary matrix URLs and hashes are recorded in the summaries; large beta matrices are not committed. `projects/postpartum_depression/ppd_methylation_audit.py` reproduces the conservative one-person-unit screens from the GEO files. The tests are exploratory in-cohort checks, not pre-registered discovery, external validation, clinical classification or a positive marker. The scientific gate remains open; the next positive direction is to resolve GSE290313's person crosswalk and prospectively predeclare a clinically meaningful endpoint with independent same-modality validation, not to rebrand null probes.
