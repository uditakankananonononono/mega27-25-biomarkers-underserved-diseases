The separate `sources/methylation_used_record_manifest.csv` tracks 139 accession records (two GSE and 137 GSM) with analytic-sample flags: only 91 GSMs are in one-person-unit exploratory tests (50 from GSE44132, 41 T0 from GSE335141). The other 46 were source/matrix-audited but deliberately not treated as independent case-control samples (five technical repeats and 41 T4 repeated draws). This is not an update to the 51-record expression manifest, and the 139 are not independent datasets.

The source crosswalks carry exact primary GEO source URLs, SHA256 of each quick SOFT response, series title and digest, platform, phenotype and person token. The GSE335141 matrix headers use array position codes rather than GSMs; each `matrix_column` was independently recovered from that GSM's `!Sample_description` and checked against the matrix. The complete public matrices were downloaded and hashed, but not stored in git due to size. Exact URL and SHA256 appear in each JSON summary. Run `PPD_GEO_CACHE=/path/with/matrix/downloads python3 projects/postpartum_depression/ppd_methylation_audit.py` to reproduce the screens; the script downloads source matrices automatically if absent. Dependencies: NumPy and SciPy.

The two cohorts differ in platform, draw timing and PPD ascertainment; no pooled meta-analysis, cross-omic inference or classifier benchmark is justified. The smallest unadjusted p-values are not discoveries because each cohort's 0.05 FDR threshold rejects none. ## GSE290313 unresolved person key

`python3 projects/postpartum_depression/audit_GSE290313_identity.py` checks the 119 previously source-verified postpartum libraries. All 119 full library tokens are unique, but there are only 80 `DE##` prefixes. Thirty-five prefixes repeat; 15 span both case and control labels. `sources/GSE290313_prefix_collision_audit.csv` names every collision. Because the `DE##` prefix is not established as a donor identifier (and its discordant labels suggest it may be reused), neither 80 nor 119 is a proven person count. These are **not** added as independent validation patients, and the 119 GSMs stay out of the conservative used manifest pending a source-backed person crosswalk.

## Clinical stratification caution for GSE44132

The antenatal sample metadata contain prepartum-depression status in addition to later PPD. Among the 50 nontechnical-control people, the source labels split as follows: future PPD yes / prepartum yes **12**, future PPD yes / prepartum no **11**; future PPD no / prepartum yes **7**, future PPD no / prepartum no **20**. The exploratory 23-versus-27 unstratified Welch screen may therefore mix prepartum depression risk and future PPD. A prospective model must handle this baseline state as a prespecified stratum or covariate; a marginal methylation difference would not prove a PPD-specific effect. Array and experimental batches are also recorded per GSM in the crosswalk and need QC before any clinical inference.

## Within-person longitudinal check (exploratory)

`paired_methylation_audit.py` paired each GSE335141 mother by source-verified patient token and compared her T4-minus-T0 beta change between 17 PPD and 24 healthy mothers. On the 880,068 complete finite probes, zero passed BH-FDR<0.05. The script and `sources/GSE335141_paired_exploratory_summary.json` retain the null. It does not interpret T4 as an early predictive measurement; it does not adjust blood-cell composition or clinical confounding, so this is not disease mechanism evidence. The observed first-ranked unadjusted probe is not a validated biomarker.
