# Pre-analysis lock: internal PPD two-CpG incremental-value test

Locked 2026-09-26 before ANY model fitting/performance on this branch. This is a secondary exploratory internal test of a **published** pair, not a new biomarker, clinical classifier, external replication, or reproduction of the 2013 authors' fitted LDA. Source and cohort caveats are in `SOURCE_RECOVERY.md`.

## Population and fixed outcome

Use GSE44132's 55 GPL13534 columns with source-matched sample crosswalk and its pinned series-matrix SHA256 `b2d715ec45c250426c8b2a4e5317bf589f581f8425d1101d9f6d752ba6dc19b9`. Exclude all five PR01-084 technical-control GSMs before any split, leaving 50 distinct patient tokens. Outcome: deposited `postpartum depression: yes` (case, n=23) versus `no` (control, n=27). Antenatal clinical baseline uses the deposited `prepartum depression: yes/no` only. Verified four cells: case/prepartum yes 12; case/prepartum no 11; control/prepartum yes 7; control/prepartum no 20. No postpartum T4 feature or GSE335141 sample enters this experiment. No DE-prefix interpretation of GSE290313 enters it.

## Locked models and split

Fixed molecular features are the *exact two GEO matrix probes* `cg21326881` and `cg00058938`, identified in the original paper, not selected on these outcomes. Three models: B0 training-fold case prevalence only; B1 L2 logistic regression on binary antenatal depression (C=1, liblinear solver); B2 same model on antenatal depression plus the two fixed methylation beta values (C=1, liblinear). For B1/B2, fit a standard scaler to training-fold columns only and apply to held-out patient; no feature selection. Outer split: leave one patient out (50 folds). Each fold emits probability for exactly one held-out person. No hyperparameter optimization or post hoc threshold tuning. Point metric: AUROC; secondary Brier score; compare B2-B1 on exactly the 50 paired held-out predictions. Fixed 0.5 threshold balanced accuracy descriptive only. Report 95% patient-bootstrap CI for AUROC difference from 2,000 resamples seeded 20260926, discarding single-class bootstrap samples and reporting discard count. Nested permutation null: 1,000 permutations of outcome labels **within prepartum-mood strata**, seed 20260926, rerun all 50-fold B1/B2 fits, empirical one-sided p=(1+count(delta_null>=delta_observed))/(1001). The null is the added molecular gain conditional on prenatal mood, not a global no-association claim. All transforms stay in training folds.

## Gate and stop logic

This test advances only to seeking truly independent, endpoint-aligned data if B2's AUROC exceeds B1 by at least 0.05 **and** the 95% bootstrap lower bound of delta exceeds zero **and** conditional permutation p<0.05; otherwise it is recorded as a failed incremental-value screen and a positive claim stops. A pass is still internal only: neither a clinical claim nor external replication. Report stratified AUROC only when both classes appear in the stratum, with wide uncertainty and no stratum-specific pass/fail threshold. Prespecify sensitivity check dropping one patient at a time from the 50 held-out predictions; report min/max delta, but do not tune based on it. No batch correction in primary model because cohort/batch overlap may make adjustment unstable; separately tabulate case/control distribution by batch and label any confounding. Negative control: repeat B2 with two fixed unrelated random CpG probe IDs selected by deterministic SHA256 ordering over matrix probe IDs **without labels**; rerun LOPO to compare qualitative magnitude, not a new gate. Do not use whole-cohort outcome-driven preprocessing, selection or imputation. Any source/mapping mismatch, nonfinite probe value, duplicate token or absent class blocks the experiment before fitting.

## Interpretation ceiling

The source's published LDA coefficients were not recovered, and the original cohort's additional pyrosequenced participants are absent from this series matrix. This is a fresh constrained robustness experiment on already-published loci and original cohort; it cannot be called external validation or a replication of the authors' numerical AUC. The locked gate is a bounded research decision, not proof of generalization.
