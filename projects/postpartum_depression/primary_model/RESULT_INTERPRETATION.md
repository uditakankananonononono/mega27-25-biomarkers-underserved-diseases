# Locked GSE44132 two-CpG internal test, caution-first interpretation

Preregistered design commit `07d7b107ae177a44ac8bd10d0bb58b66af4238c1`; source matrix SHA256 `b2d715ec45c250426c8b2a4e5317bf589f581f8425d1101d9f6d752ba6dc19b9`. On 50 unique patients (23 future PPD, 27 control), the fixed two CpGs plus prenatal-mood LOPO logistic model had AUROC 0.757. The fold-retrained prenatal-mood-only LOPO logistic model had AUROC 0.386; raw prenatal-mood binary score (same 50 persons) yields AUROC 0.631. Thus the apparent delta **0.370** against a fold-retrained baseline is misleading as evidence of molecular gain: leave-one-out training prevalence changes *because of the withheld label*, making the intercept-only LOPO AUROC literally 0.0 and the mood-only predictor rank within each mood stratum in reverse order (within-stratum AUC 0.0). This is a validation-design artifact, not proof that a mood-only model is worse than random. Descriptively, molecular AUROC exceeds the raw mood score by 0.126, but this was not the preregistered comparator, and no respecified gate can be claimed retroactively.

The locked script produced a 95% bootstrap interval 0.189–0.560 and within-mood permutation p=0.001 for B2 minus the artifact-prone B1; these quantify that exact flawed comparison and do not rescue it. Crucially, B2 performance is directionally inconsistent by prenatal-mood stratum: euthymic 11 cases/20 controls AUC 0.877, depressed 12 cases/7 controls AUC 0.345. The consultation and prereg both named cross-stratum coherence as a validity check; `gate_pass: true` in the raw JSON only applied three numeric criteria and is **overridden as scientifically NOT PASSED** here. The prior paper explicitly reported opposite direction in the depressed stratum. This result is a negative boundary for a universal two-locus predictor, not a clinical win.

No external prediction cohort was tested, no original published coefficients were recovered, and the study is the original historical source cohort. We preserve the script, held-out per-person predictions, raw numbers and failed interpretation in the ledger; do not call the 0.757 AUROC an independent benchmark pass. Next valid path needs a proper fixed mood baseline or stratified score with a fresh prereg amendment and independent endpoint-matched cohort. Do not tune this same cohort to rescue a positive.
