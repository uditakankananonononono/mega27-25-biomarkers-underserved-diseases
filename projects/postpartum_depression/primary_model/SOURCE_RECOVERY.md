The supplement's Table 1 says original euthymic group 13 PPD/22 non-PPD and depressed replication group 12/7. Its Figure 3 caption says forward stepwise linear discriminant analysis chose the two loci and predicted held-out group. These numbers differ from our GEO matrix's 50 unique nontechnical analytic people: future PPD/mood-depressed 12, PPD/mood-euthymic 11, control/depressed 7, control/euthymic 20. The main article says three extra women were pyrosequenced but not microarray-profiled; one additional difference in totals still requires exact participant mapping before reproducing an original published AUC. Do not equate the paper's N=54 total with our N=50.

A legitimate full-text alternate route succeeded via NCBI BioC open full text: https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/research/bionlp/RESTful/pmcoa.cgi/BioC_json/PMC7039252/unicode . It reports original forward-stepwise selection among 1578 mouse E2 DMRs overlapping human probes, linear discriminant analysis maximizing discovery AUC, loci cg21326881 (HP1BP3) and cg00058938 (TTC9B), then prediction in the prepartum-depressed replication sample. It states methylation-only AUC 0.92 discovery/0.90 replication for selected array samples, and pyrosequencing AUC 0.87 euthymic and **0.12 opposite direction** in depressed group. A separate later model with cell-type ratio and HP1BP3 interaction reports bootstrap AUC 0.82 on 51 women, and a smaller CBC subgroup AUC 0.96 on N=17. These are published in-cohort claims, not our reproduced results or robust external validation. Original LDA numerical coefficients and exact training memberships are not furnished in the public article or the supplementary text; the Figure 3 schematic is visual, not a coefficient table. The later regression displays beta coefficients but requires cell-type ratios not present in the GEO crosswalk. No exact published prediction function has been reconstructed.
