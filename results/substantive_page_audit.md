# Times manuscript research-content page audit, 2026-09-25

Input: `paper/manuscript-times.pdf`, rendered with embedded Times New Roman body font. `scripts/audit_substantive_pages.py` extracts text and applies a conservative 250-word-per-page threshold to pages 3-26. It excludes the title and contents (1-2), sparse split table (4), figure/table-heavy pages (7, 15, 16), the reference/tool appendix (27-30), and pages failing the word floor. Twenty pages qualify: 3, 5, 6, 8-14, 17-26. These contain methods, mathematical derivations, quantitative results, negative findings, cohort cautions, and interpretation, not a repeated filler passage. Page 3 and the P27 result pages 25-26 were visually checked after final rendering; other research pages had been checked during earlier builds. The automated floor is a reproducible proxy for substantive content, not peer review or a guarantee of scientific validity. The benchmark-or-discovery gate remains open.
