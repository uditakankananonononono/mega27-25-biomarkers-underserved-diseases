# Times manuscript research-content page audit, 2026-09-25

Input: `paper/manuscript-times.pdf`, 32 rendered pages with embedded Times New Roman body font. `scripts/audit_substantive_pages.py` extracts text and applies a conservative 250-word-per-page floor to pages 3-28, excluding front matter (1-2), sparse split/figure/table pages (4, 7, 8, 16), references and tool appendix (29-32), and any other page falling short of the word floor. Twenty-two pages qualify: 3, 5-6, 9-15, 17-28. They contain methods, mathematical derivations, quantitative results, negative findings, cohort cautions and interpretation. Pages 3 and 26-28 were visually inspected after the latest rendering; other research pages were inspected during prior builds. This word floor is a reproducible content proxy, not peer review or scientific validation. The benchmark-or-discovery gate remains open.
